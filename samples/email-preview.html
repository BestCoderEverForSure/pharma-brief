3. Employers signal retreat from GLP-1 weight-loss coverage

What it means: The headline demand surge for obesity drugs masks a brewing affordability crisis in employer-sponsored plans. Lenses: Commercial & market access — payer dynamics are shifting faster than many expected. Financial — pricing pressure is the key risk for the obesity growth thesis. Strategic — oral GLP-1 drugs (orforglipron, amycretin) may improve adherence but won't solve the payer math.

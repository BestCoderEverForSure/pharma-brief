### 1. [Moderna's mRNA Flu Shot Wins US Approval in FDA Turnaround](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-06/moderna-s-mrna-flu-shot-wins-us-approval-in-fda-turnaround) {major}

### 2. [U.S. FDA Approves Takeda’s ORZEYFUL™ (oveporexton), the First and Only Medicine to Treat the Underlying Cause of Narcolepsy Type 1](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260805646142/en/U.S.-FDA-Approves-Takeda%E2%80%99s-ORZEYFUL%E2%84%A2-oveporexton-the-First-and-Only-Medicine-to-Treat-the-Underlying-Cause-of-Narcolepsy-Type-1)
**What happened:** Takeda's Oveporexton (marketed as Orzeyful), an orexin receptor agonist, received FDA approval as the first and only medicine to treat the underlying cause of Narcolepsy Type 1 (NT1) [22] [81].
**What it means:** This approval introduces a novel class of treatment for NT1, moving beyond symptomatic management to address the disease's root cause. It positions Takeda to capture a significant share of the narcolepsy market and could set a new standard of care, benefiting patients who previously had limited options [14] [35].
**Lenses:** *Scientific* - Targets underlying disease mechanism. *Commercial* - Creates a new market segment for Takeda.

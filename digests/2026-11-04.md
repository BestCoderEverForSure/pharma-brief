### 1. [Novo Nordisk Hikes 2026 Outlook on Strong Wegovy Pill Sales](https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/novo-nordisk-hikes-2026-outlook-again-wegovy-pill-beats-forecasts-2026-11-04/)

### 2. [FDA Grants Priority Review to UniQure's Huntington's Gene Therapy](https://www.huntingtonsnewstoday.com/news/2026/11/03/fda-grants-priority-review-gene-therapy-huntingtons/)
**What happened:** The FDA has accepted UniQure's Biologics License Application (BLA) for its one-time Huntington's disease gene therapy, granting it priority review. An advisory committee meeting is anticipated as part of the review process [3] [28] [44] [69].
**What it means:** Priority review status signals the FDA's recognition of this gene therapy's potential to offer a significant improvement over existing treatments for this devastating neurodegenerative condition with high unmet need. The likely advisory committee meeting indicates the FDA's intent for a thorough public discussion of the complex data, which is common for novel gene therapies in severe diseases.
**Lenses:** *Regulatory* - Expedited review pathway for a rare disease gene therapy. · *Scientific* - Potential for a one-time treatment in a progressive neurological disorder.

### 3. [Servier's Muscular Dystrophy Drug Sevasemten Fails Phase 3 Trial](https://www.statnews.com/2026/11/03/servier-muscular-dystrophy-drug-fails-first-major-test/)
**What happened:** Servier announced a clinical setback for its drug sevasemten, which missed its muscular dystrophy endpoints in a trial [4] [74].
**What it means:** This failure is a blow to Servier and to patients suffering from this form of muscular dystrophy, an area with limited treatment options. It highlights the inherent risks and challenges in developing therapies for complex rare diseases, where even promising candidates can falter in late-stage trials, impacting patient hope and investor confidence in the pipeline.
**Lenses:** *Scientific* - Disappointing clinical trial outcome in a high-need area. · *Strategic* - Forces Servier to reassess its muscular dystrophy pipeline and investment.

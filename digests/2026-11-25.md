### 2. Roche's oral SERD wins second FDA approval for early-stage breast cancer
**What happened:** Roche's oral selective estrogen receptor degrader (SERD) received its second FDA clearance in a week, this time approved for use in early-stage breast cancer after surgery [2] [5].
**What it means:** This approval opens a large market opportunity for Roche, which has positioned the drug as a potential multibillion-dollar product [2] [5]. It strengthens Roche's oncology portfolio and sets the stage for significant commercial uptake in a broad patient population.

### 3. BridgeBio Pharma's muscular dystrophy drug receives FDA approval
**What happened:** The FDA approved BridgeBio Pharma's drug for muscular dystrophy [1]. Wall Street analysts project blockbuster sales, with a price tag estimated at nearly $1 million per year for the medicine [1].
**What it means:** This approval provides a new treatment option for patients with muscular dystrophy, a rare disease, and represents a significant commercial opportunity for BridgeBio due to its high price and potential to improve patients' ability to walk and breathe [1]. The high price point could also draw scrutiny amid ongoing drug pricing debates.

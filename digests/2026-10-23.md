### 2. [Moderna, Merck shares fall on eagerly anticipated cancer vaccine results](https://statnews.com/2026/10/22/moderna-merck-shares-fall-on-eagerly-anticipated-cancer-vaccine-results/)
**What happened:** A combination of Moderna's mRNA cancer vaccine and Merck's Keytruda (pembrolizumab) cut the risk of melanoma recurrence or death by 42% in a Phase 3 study [2] [133]. Despite meeting Wall Street expectations, shares for both companies fell in a "sell-the-news" reaction [2].
**What it means:** This data reinforces the potential of personalized mRNA cancer vaccines, offering a new treatment paradigm for high-risk melanoma patients [133]. However, the market reaction suggests high expectations were already priced in, highlighting the challenge of exceeding investor anticipation even with strong clinical data [2].
**Lenses:** *Scientific* - Validates mRNA vaccine approach in oncology. *Commercial* - Strong efficacy for a high-value indication, but market access and pricing will be key. *Financial* - "Sell-the-news" reaction despite positive data, indicating high pre-release valuation.

### 3. [Biohaven’s previously rejected rare disease drug to get fresh FDA review](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/biohaven-vyglxia-troriluzole-fda-resubmission-ataxia/697779/)
**What happened:** The FDA has accepted a new submission for Biohaven's Vyglxia (troriluzole), a drug for spinocerebellar ataxia that was previously rejected by the agency [3] [23] [49]. This news caused Biohaven's stock to surge by 25% [34].
**What it means:** This represents a second chance for a rare disease therapy and signals a potential shift in FDA's approach or a stronger data package from Biohaven. The market's strong positive reaction underscores the value placed on regulatory progress for orphan drugs, particularly after an initial setback [3] [34].
**Lenses:** *Regulatory* - FDA re-evaluation of a previously rejected drug. *Financial* - Significant stock surge reflecting renewed investor confidence. *Commercial* - Potential for a new treatment option in an underserved rare disease.

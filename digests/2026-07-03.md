### 2. [Federal Judge Blocks Colorado’s First-of-its-Kind Price Cap on an Amgen Drug](https://www.statnews.com/2026/07/02/us-judge-blocks-colorados-first-of-its-kind-price-cap-on-an-amgen-drug/)

**What it means:** This ruling temporarily halts Colorado's first-of-its-kind attempt to set a state-level price cap on a specific drug, which could have created a precedent for other states. **Lenses:** *Regulatory & Legal* - The decision challenges the authority of state panels to directly cap drug prices, impacting future state-level pricing initiatives. · *Financial* - Amgen avoids a direct price reduction on a key revenue driver, preserving its commercial model in Colorado and potentially other states.

It signals continued innovation and competition in the IPF market, which currently includes established drugs like Esbriet and Boehringer Ingelheim's Ofev.
**Lenses:** *Financial* - A large funding round provides significant capital for Celea to advance its pipeline. · *Strategic* - Celea aims to carve out a niche in a market with existing blockbusters by offering an "improved version," potentially challenging current market leaders.

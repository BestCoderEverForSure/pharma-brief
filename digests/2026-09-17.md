### 2. AstraZeneca's Enhertu Gains NHS Coverage in England {major}
**What happened:** AstraZeneca's breast cancer drug Enhertu (trastuzumab deruxtecan) will now be available on England's National Health Service (NHS) for HER2-low breast cancer, following a pricing deal influenced by the broader US-UK pharma pact [6] [232] [241].
**What it means:** This represents a significant market access victory for AstraZeneca, expanding the drug's reach in a key European market and demonstrating how international drug pricing agreements can facilitate patient access to innovative therapies [6] [241].
**Lenses:** *Commercial & market access* - Broadens patient access and revenue potential for Enhertu in a major market. · *Regulatory & legal* - Illustrates the impact of bilateral pricing agreements on national formulary decisions. · *Strategic & competitive* - Strengthens AstraZeneca's oncology portfolio and competitive position.

### 3. Sling Therapeutics Raises $123M for Oral Thyroid Eye Disease Drug
**What happened:** Sling Therapeutics secured $123 million in Series C financing to fund a late-stage study for its experimental thyroid eye disease (TED) pill, an oral IGF-1R blocker [3] [12] [124] [144] [216].
**What it means:** This funding positions Sling to potentially introduce the first oral alternative to existing injectable treatments like Amgen's Tepezza and Viridian's Lumvoa, which could offer greater convenience for patients and disrupt the current market landscape [3] [12] [144].
**Lenses:** *Commercial & market access* - Potential to capture significant market share by offering a more convenient dosage form. · *Strategic & competitive* - Poses a direct challenge to established injectable therapies in the TED market. · *Financial & capital markets* - Significant capital infusion to de-risk a late-stage asset.

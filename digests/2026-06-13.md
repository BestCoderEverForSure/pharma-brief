## Top Stories

### 1. [FDA approves belzutifan/pembrolizumab for adjuvant kidney cancer](https://www.onclive.com/view/fda-approves-adjuvant-belzutifan-plus-pembrolizumab-in-ccrcc)
**What happened:** The FDA approved Merck's Welireg (belzutifan, a HIF-2α inhibitor) with Keytruda (pembrolizumab) as adjuvant treatment for high-risk clear cell renal cell carcinoma (ccRCC) after nephrectomy. [28-30, 32-33, 38, 44, 55-56, 84] Separately, the FDA granted Roche's Tecentriq combo priority review for stage III dMMR/MSI-H colon cancer. [12] [40]
**What it means:** This is the first adjuvant HIF-2α regimen in kidney cancer, expanding belzutifan beyond its original VHL-disease indication and setting a new post-surgery standard for high-risk patients.
**Lenses:** *Regulatory* — cleared without a panel. *Strategic* — Merck locks in another renal indication and extends Keytruda's combination runway. *Commercial* — broadens adjuvant market for both agents.

### 2. [FDA expands teplizumab to stage 3 T1D; approves capivasertib combo for prostate cancer](https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/fda-approves-expanded-use-sanofis-type-1-diabetes-drug-2026-06-12/)
**What happened:** Sanofi's Tzield (teplizumab) received accelerated FDA approval for stage 3 (symptomatic) type 1 diabetes in children, expanding from its stage 2 prevention label. Separately, the FDA approved AstraZeneca's capivasertib with abiraterone and prednisone for PTEN-deficient metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer (mHSPC) — a decision that came after an advisory committee meeting. [13, 15-16, 21-23, 43, 45, 70, 106]
**What it means:** Teplizumab becomes the first disease-modifying therapy available at the point of clinical T1D diagnosis, not just pre-symptomatic screening — a large population expansion. The capivasertib approval gives AZ a genomically targeted entry (~40% of mHSPC patients) in a competitive prostate cancer market.
**Lenses:** *Scientific* — teplizumab's anti-CD3 mechanism preserves beta-cell function in clinical disease; capivasertib inhibits the PI3K/AKT pathway in PTEN-loss tumours. *Regulatory* — both via accelerated pathways; capivasertib's AdCom debate adds risk context. *Commercial* — Sanofi gains a dramatically larger T1D patient pool; AZ adds a branded targeted combo to its prostate franchise.

### 3. [Novo Nordisk's oral Wegovy wins UK approval](https://www.pharmaceutical-technology.com/news/novo-nordisk-comeback-stirs-as-wegovy-pill-wins-uk-approval/)
**What happened:** The UK MHRA approved a once-daily oral formulation of semaglutide (Wegovy) for weight management. Digital health platforms already report lengthy patient waitlists. [9]
**What it means:** The first oral GLP-1 approved for obesity anywhere, offering a needle-free alternative that could expand the treatment population. It strengthens Novo's position against injectable and oral competitors, including Lilly's orforglipron and Amgen's MariTide.
**Lenses:** *Competitive* — directly pressures Lilly's oral orforglipron programme to demonstrate Phase 3 superiority or equivalence. *Commercial* — oral formulation could unlock injection-averse patients if reimbursement follows. *Regulatory* — UK first; building block for submissions in the EU and US.

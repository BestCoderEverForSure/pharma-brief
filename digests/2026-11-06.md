## Top Stories

### 1. [J&J's Rybrevant Faspro gains FDA approval for head and neck cancer](https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/fda-grants-jj-accelerated-approval-head-neck-cancer-treatment-2026-11-05/) {major}
**What happened:** Johnson & Johnson (J&J) received accelerated FDA approval for Rybrevant Faspro (amivantamab and hyaluronidase-lpuj) as the first and only dual-targeted therapy for advanced head and neck cancer [16] [55] [64]. This marks an expansion for the drug, previously approved for non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) [20] [43].
**What it means:** This approval provides a new treatment option for a difficult-to-treat cancer, potentially establishing a new standard of care after a decade without new options [22]. For J&J, it diversifies Rybrevant's market beyond lung cancer, strengthening its oncology portfolio and competitive position [20] [104].
**Lenses:** *Commercial* - Expands market opportunity for Rybrevant beyond its initial lung cancer indication. *Strategic* - Reinforces J&J's leadership in oncology with a novel dual-targeted therapy. *Regulatory* - Accelerated approval highlights the unmet need in head and neck cancer.

### 2. [Medicare to cut billions from 340B, expand site-neutral payments in 2027](https://www.statnews.com/2026/11/05/medicare-will-cut-billions-from-340b-expand-site-neutral-payments-in-2027/)
**What happened:** Regulators finalized a rule that will reduce payments for drugs in the 340B program and equalize some payments between different care sites starting in 2027 [1]. Hospitals have expressed strong opposition to these changes [1].
**What it means:** This policy shift will significantly impact hospital finances, particularly those serving a high proportion of low-income patients, as they rely on 340B discounts to support services [1]. For pharmaceutical companies, it could influence drug pricing strategies and market access dynamics, especially for drugs heavily utilized within the 340B program.
**Lenses:** *Regulatory* - Finalized rule reflects a continued push by Medicare to control drug spending and standardize reimbursement. *Financial* - Hospitals face billions in payment cuts, potentially affecting their ability to provide care and invest in new technologies. *Commercial* - Pharma companies may see shifts in demand and pricing pressure within the 340B segment.

### 3. [Novartis cites cases of previously unknown tumors possibly tied to its SMA gene therapy](https://www.statnews.com/2026/11/05/novartis-zolgensma-tumors/)
**What happened:** Novartis has acknowledged seven previously unknown instances where tumors were found in children treated with its spinal muscular atrophy (SMA) gene therapy, Zolgensma [6].
**What it means:** This development raises significant safety concerns for Zolgensma, a high-profile gene therapy [6]. It also casts a shadow over the broader gene therapy landscape, potentially impacting investor confidence and the development timelines for other gene therapies as regulators may demand more extensive long-term safety data.
**Lenses:** *Scientific & Clinical* - Highlights the complex and sometimes unforeseen long-term safety profiles of novel gene therapies. *Regulatory & Legal* - Could prompt further investigations by health authorities and potentially lead to new warnings or restrictions. *Financial & Capital Markets* - May negatively impact Novartis's stock and the valuation of other gene therapy developers.

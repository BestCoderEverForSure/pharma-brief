**Lenses:** *Regulatory* - Overcoming previous rejections and FDA staff concerns is a rare feat, suggesting a strong patient need and compelling panel review. *Financial* - Replimune's stock surged on the news, reflecting renewed investor confidence and the potential for a new market entry [103].

### 2. [J&J Nabs Option for *In Vivo* CAR-T Maker Sail Bio for Up to $2.6B](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/johnson-johnson-sail-bio-car-t-in-vivo-autoimmune/722221/)
**What happened:** Johnson & Johnson (J&J) has secured an option to acquire Sail Bio, a Flagship Pioneering-backed startup developing *in vivo* CAR-T (chimeric antigen receptor T-cell) therapies, for nearly $2.6 billion, including an upfront payment of $785 million [4] [95] [140].
**What it means:** This deal positions J&J as the latest major drugmaker to invest heavily in *in vivo* CAR-T technology, which aims to deliver gene-editing tools directly into the body to reprogram T-cells, potentially offering a more accessible and scalable treatment for autoimmune conditions than traditional CAR-T [4] [166]. It signals a strategic bet on the next generation of cell therapies.
**Lenses:** *Strategic* - J&J is expanding its cell therapy portfolio beyond oncology into autoimmune diseases, a growing area of interest for CAR-T. *Financial* - The multi-billion dollar option demonstrates big pharma's willingness to pay significant sums for innovative platform technologies with potential for broad application [4].

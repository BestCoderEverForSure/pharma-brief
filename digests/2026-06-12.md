### 3. Employers signal retreat from GLP-1 weight-loss coverage
**What happened:** A growing number of employers plan to drop coverage of GLP-1 drugs for weight loss next year, even as the number of people taking the medications continues to rise sharply [3].
**What it means:** The headline demand surge for obesity drugs masks a brewing affordability crisis in employer-sponsored plans. If coverage erodes meaningfully, it could temper volume growth for Lilly's Zepbound and Novo Nordisk's Wegovy, forcing the companies to lean more on list-price concessions or direct-to-consumer models.
**Lenses:** *Commercial & market access* — payer dynamics are shifting faster than many expected. *Financial* — pricing pressure is the key risk for the obesity growth thesis. *Strategic* — oral GLP-1 drugs (orforglipron, amycretin) may improve adherence but won't solve the payer math.

### 4. Novartis' del-brax (Avidity asset) hits Phase 1/2 endpoint in muscle-wasting disease

**Lenses:** *Scientific & clinical* — early but encouraging data for a novel modality. *Strategic* — justifies Novartis' large acquisition price and strengthens its neuromuscular pipeline. *Technology* — RNA conjugates could be a platform play beyond this single indication.

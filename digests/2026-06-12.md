## Top Stories

### 1. [Takeda's zasocitinib tops Bristol Myers' Sotyktu in head-to-head psoriasis trial](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/takeda-zasocitinib-sotyktu-psoriasis-trial/822500/) {major}
**What happened:** Takeda's oral TYK2 inhibitor zasocitinib met its primary endpoint versus BMS's Sotyktu (deucravacitinib) in a head-to-head Phase 3 psoriasis study, showing superior efficacy. [4]
**What it means:** The readout vindicates Takeda's $4B bet on zasocitinib (acquired via Nimbus in 2021) and hands it a potential commercial edge in the fast-growing oral psoriasis/TYK2 market. Sotyktu was the first TYK2 inhibitor approved but has faced uptake headwinds.
**Lenses:** *Strategic* - Takeda leapfrogs BMS in TYK2 positioning, potentially capturing first-line oral systemic share · *Commercial* - superiority claims support premium pricing and payer differentiation · *Scientific* - validates TYK2 pseudokinase inhibition as a differentiated mechanism with better safety than JAKs.

### 2. [Summit pulls $500M share sale a day after announcing it](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/summit-therapeutics-pulls-500-million-share-sale-pd1-vegf-ivonescimab/822619/)
**What happened:** Summit Therapeutics cancelled its planned $500M share offering, citing "market conditions," one day after announcing it. [1]
**What it means:** The abrupt reversal signals investor resistance to dilution despite the company's promising PD-1/VEGF bispecific ivonescimab. Summit's stock has struggled to break out following prior data milestones, and the withdrawal suggests management misjudged market appetite.
**Lenses:** *Financial* - dilutive financing pulled; signals weak equity market reception for Summit's risk profile · *Strategic* - ivonescimab's commercial path (vs. Keytruda + chemo in China, global plans uncertain) may need non-dilutive partners · *Competitive* - Merck's Keytruda and Roche's Tecentriq remain formidable with established IO/VEGF combos.

### 3. [Oral Wegovy FDA win reframes Novo Nordisk growth and longevity story](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMingFBVV95cUxQaHhoZ3hvSkNVazJzMU1hcjdrMUlZTl84bnJYbFFPV0w3aE9vbVRfQ3Y1Vi00eEFLUXEyaF9LV200eEtSejd6NnNjMkIycUNmS2pUZ0ZXYW5kMURReG5DOGhfTjRGLV9JOFYzc3V0a0hHVHVZcXFhVjFxZVRMRmdHQ0NkVkduN05IUFJ5a3Q1YUY0NWpqejZCeHpLRlpzQQ?oc=5)
**What happened:** The FDA approved oral semaglutide (Wegovy) for weight management, marking the first oral GLP-1 for obesity. [28] This follows the injectable Wegovy approval and expands Novo's addressable patient pool.
**What it means:** Oral Wegovy opens a larger market (patients averse to injections) and extends Novo's GLP-1 franchise life. It also puts direct competitive pressure on Lilly's oral orforglipron (Foundayo), which is expected to file with FDA around end-June.
**Lenses:** *Financial* - protects Novo's market cap from erosion by injectable-only competitors · *Strategic* - first-mover advantage in oral GLP-1 obesity, but Lilly's orforglipron may have superior efficacy · *Commercial* - dosing compliance and GI tolerability still open questions vs. injectables.

## 🎯 Lilly Watch

- **Oral Wegovy approval** - Novo Nordisk's oral semaglutide is now approved for obesity, directly competing with Lilly's oral orforglipron (Foundayo), which is expected to file with FDA around end-June via the PreCheck pilot. Lilly still holds the efficacy edge from its Phase 3 readout (ATTAIN-1), but Novo has first-to-market and manufacturing scale advantages. [28]
- **FDA steps up crackdown on compounded GLP-1s** - increased enforcement against compounded semaglutide/tirzepatide copies could benefit branded products by squeezing grey-market supply. [91]
- **More employers planning to drop GLP-1 weight-loss coverage next year** - survey data shows increasing employer reluctance to cover expensive weight-loss drugs, threatening the volume trajectory for both Novo and Lilly's obesity franchises. [7]
- **FDA warns of kidney stone/injury risk with OTC orlistat** - not a GLP-1, but any safety noise around OTC weight-loss drugs could reinforce preference for physician-supervised branded options. [104]

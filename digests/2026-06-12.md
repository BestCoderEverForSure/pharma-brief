## Top Stories

### 1. [Parabilis sets record with $670M biotech IPO](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/parabilis-biotech-ipo-price-helicon-peptides-cancer-verdine/822397/)
**What happened:** Parabilis Medicines priced the largest-ever venture-backed biotech IPO, raising $670M. Shares jumped 58% on debut [7][41][53][140].
**What it means:** The deal extends a hot 2026 IPO streak — 12 drug startups have now raised over $4.1B combined. The record was broken twice in as many months, signalling robust investor appetite for platform-driven oncology and peptide therapeutics [7][88][128].
**Lenses:** *Financial* — strongest biotech IPO market since 2021, large oversubscription validates investor confidence in early-stage platforms. *Strategic* — the "record IPO" label attracts more private capital into the space, potentially compressing late-stage private valuations.

### 2. [GSK to buy Nuvalent for $10.6B](https://www.statnews.com/2026/06/09/gsk-nuvalent-acquistion-oncology-lung-cancer-zidesamtinib-neladalkib/?utm_campaign=rss)
**What happened:** GSK agreed to acquire cancer drug developer Nuvalent for $10.6B, adding targeted therapies zidesamtinib (ROS1+ NSCLC) and neladalkib (ALK+ NSCLC) [20][21].
**What it means:** A sizable addition to GSK's oncology rebuild. Zidesamtinib has a September PDUFA date (per catalyst calendar), making this a near-term value catalyst. The deal continues a busy M&A cycle — seven multibillion-dollar acquisitions occurred in May 2026 alone [34].
**Lenses:** *Strategic* — GSK deepens oncology after earlier pipeline rebuilding. *Regulatory* — zidesamtinib PDUFA on 2026-09-18 is now the first key milestone of the acquisition. *Financial* — $10.6B is substantial but reflects Nuvalent's strong late-stage position.

### 3. [Takeda's $4B TYK2 drug tops Bristol Myers' Sotyktu in head-to-head test](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/takeda-zasocitinib-sotyktu-psoriasis-trial/822500/)
**What happened:** Takeda's oral TYK2 inhibitor zasocitinib beat BMS's Sotyktu in a head-to-head psoriasis trial, supporting Takeda's claim of superiority [4].
**What it means:** A defining moment for Takeda's immunology franchise. If confirmed, zasocitinib could capture significant share in the oral psoriasis market, which BMS had been building with Sotyktu. Notably, an AI-designed pill also recently outperformed an approved Bristol drug, adding to the competitive pressure on BMS's portfolio [137].
**Lenses:** *Scientific & clinical* — head-to-head superiority is a high bar; positive data strengthens the probability of regulatory and commercial success. *Commercial* — oral psoriasis is a growing market; a "best-in-class" label matters for payer positioning.

### 4. [Novartis RNA drug acquired in $12B Avidity deal notches a trial win](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/novartis-del-brax-avidity-trial-data/822628/)
**What happened:** Del-brax, an antisense oligonucleotide conjugate for a muscle-wasting disease, met its primary endpoint in a Phase 1/2 study. Novartis acquired Avidity in a $12B deal earlier this year [3].
**What it means:** Early validation of a major M&A bet. The RNA-targeting platform was Avidity's core draw; this readout supports the thesis and de-risks the deal. Separately, Novartis is doubling down on its molecular glue strategy, indicating the company is making a broad platform push in novel therapeutic modalities [12].
**Lenses:** *Scientific* — first clinical proof-of-concept for the Avidity platform post-acquisition. *Financial* — positive data protects the deal's ROI. *Strategic* — Novartis is placing multiple platform bets (RNA, molecular glues) simultaneously.

## 🎯 Lilly Watch
- **Retatrutide safety data** — Lilly presented new safety and tolerability data on its triple-agonist obesity drug at the American Diabetes Association meeting. The data provide detail on the profile of this next-generation candidate [24][26].
- **Lilly-AlzeCure $1B Alzheimer's deal** — Lilly licensed AlzeCure's ACD680 in a deal potentially worth over $1B. If successful, the asset would deepen Lilly's Alzheimer's franchise beyond Kisunla (anti-amyloid) into a novel mechanism [44].
- **Competitor GLP-1 data flood** — key obesity competitors all released data this week: AstraZeneca's GLP-1 pill showed mid-stage promise with a "sprawling" late-stage program now planned [8][22]; Pfizer's monthly obesity drug (berobenatide, acquired from Metsera) showed continued potential for monthly dosing [27]; Boehringer Ingelheim's survodutide showed liver fat reduction but less impressive weight loss [25]. Separately, Novo Nordisk was described as "underwhelmed" by a drug it once fought Pfizer for, suggesting some pipeline tension in its post-Wegovy portfolio [23].
- **So what for Lilly**: The week reinforced that the oral GLP-1 race is intensifying (AZ, Pfizer, Novo, Boehringer all moving) — but no competitor data yet looks directly threatening to Lilly's tirzepatide franchise, and retatrutide's continued progress keeps Lilly's next-gen pipeline on track.

## Top Stories

### 1. [Takeda's $4B TYK2 drug tops Bristol Myers' Sotyktu in head-to-head test](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/takeda-zasocitinib-sotyktu-psoriasis-trial/822500/)
**What happened:** Takeda announced its oral TYK2 inhibitor zasocitinib beat BMS's Sotyktu (deucravacitinib) in a head-to-head Phase 3 psoriasis trial. [4]
**What it means:** This validates Takeda's decision to pay $4B for Nimbus's TYK2 program and positions it to challenge BMS's first-in-class drug, which had a slow launch.
**Lenses:** *Strategic* — Takeda now has a clear differentiation story vs. BMS in the oral psoriasis market. *Commercial* — head-to-head superiority could drive formulary wins and patient share. *Financial* — protects Takeda's return on its largest recent acquisition.

### 2. [Novartis RNA drug acquired in $12B Avidity deal notches a trial win](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/novartis-del-brax-avidity-trial-data/822628/)
**What happened:** Novartis's del-brax, an antisense oligonucleotide conjugate (a type of RNA-targeting therapeutic) for the muscle-wasting disease myotonic dystrophy type 1, met its primary endpoint in a Phase 1/2 study. [3]
**What it means:** The data supports Novartis's rationale for the $12B Avidity Biosciences acquisition — and validates the oligonucleotide conjugate platform for muscle diseases.
**Lenses:** *Scientific* — positive Phase 1/2 data in a disease with no approved therapies. *Strategic* — early validation of a $12B M&A bet. *Financial* — de-risks a major capital allocation decision.

[Amgen Reevaluates Tavneos Data as Drug Faces FDA Scrutiny](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMisgFBVV95cUxNbW1QWjdERC1QSThuMW5TTG1zSU90OFZ2Qm9BSk1mWXdMd1RMdHAwck9CWXphNmhPWUFGQ05tVWRwLWZsWEVhMkVkRWpDYldFYjRydTVscFhzQkYxaHJYRndZaWZ1cUY1WXJWbWRuYTNMZ29ILXpoSExKOUtkNVZTN2V1Y3pjV0w5OF9rOVN1Z2JLc3BhaDEtTHJuTjdESDl3eHR0Z2RGQWdlV3V2b0ZKMlB3/)
**What happened:** Bloomberg reports Amgen is reevaluating clinical data for Tavneos (avacopan), its ANCA-associated vasculitis drug, as it faces FDA scrutiny. [13]
**What it means:** This adds regulatory uncertainty around a drug Amgen acquired through its $13.4B ChemoCentryx buyout; any label restrictions or safety concerns would hit a still-ramping launch.
**Lenses:** *Regulatory* — active FDA scrutiny could lead to labeling changes. *Financial* — questions the return on a major acquisition. *Strategic* — a setback here would leave Amgen thinner in inflammation.

### 4. [Summit pulls $500M share sale a day after announcing it](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/summit-therapeutics-pulls-500-million-share-sale-pd1-vegf-ivonescimab/822619/)
**What happened:** Summit Therapeutics withdrew a $500M share offering just one day after announcing it, citing "market conditions." Its shares have struggled to break out despite data milestones for ivonescimab (PD-1/VEGF bispecific). [1]
**What it means:** This is a confidence signal: Summit's stock isn't reflecting its perceived pipeline progress, making large equity raises unattractive.
**Lenses:** *Financial* — weak equity market reception despite promising data. *Strategic* — may push Summit toward partnership or alternative financing. *Competitive* — gives Merck (Keytruda) breathing room in the PD-1/VEGF space.

## 🎯 Lilly Watch

- **Compounded GLP-1 crackdown** — FDA is stepping up enforcement against compounded versions of GLP-1 drugs, which have proliferated amid shortages. This directly protects Lilly's Mounjaro/Zepbound franchise from copycat competition. [23]
- **Employer coverage squeeze** — More employers plan to drop GLP-1 coverage for weight loss in 2027 as utilization grows, a STAT+ report finds. This is a commercial headwind for both Lilly and Novo Nordisk — insurance access is the single biggest volume lever for the obesity market. [5]
- **FDA warns on OTC orlistat** — The agency warned about kidney stone and acute kidney injury risks with orlistat (Alli/Xenical), the older OTC weight-loss drug. Not a direct Lilly threat but keeps safety scrutiny on the entire weight-loss drug class. [29]
- **AI-designed oral drug beats BMS** — An AI-designed pill (unnamed in the report) outperformed an approved Bristol Myers drug, per Investor's Business Daily. Worth watching as Lilly pursues oral orforglipron — AI-designed oral GLP-1s could become competitive. [33]
- **New insulin option for kids** — FDA approved a new way for children with diabetes to take insulin. Peripheral relevance but underscores the expanding diabetes-device/drug ecosystem Lilly competes in. [16]

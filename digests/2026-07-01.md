## Top Stories

### 1. [Anthropic Launches AI for Pharma, Plans Own Drug Development](https://statnews.com/2026/06/30/anthropic-releases-claude-science-a-product-aimed-at-researchers-the-pharma-industry/)
**What happened:** AI company Anthropic has launched "Claude Science," an application optimizing its large language model for scientific research and drugmakers, and simultaneously announced its intention to begin developing drugs of its own [7] [8] [10] [210] [215] [230] [235].
**What it means:** This marks a significant move by a major AI player to both provide tools to the pharmaceutical industry and directly enter the competitive drug discovery space, potentially disrupting traditional R&D models and intensifying the race for AI-driven innovation [5] [6] [7] [247].
**Lenses:** *Strategic* - Tech giant becomes direct competitor/partner · *Technology* - AI's expanding role in drug discovery · *Financial* - New revenue streams for AI, R&D cost implications.

### 2. [FDA Approves Orca Bio's Tregzi for Blood Cancer Patients](https://statnews.com/2026/07/01/fda-approves-orca-bios-t-cell-therapy-for-blood-cancer-patients/)
**What happened:** The FDA has approved Orca Bio's T-cell therapy, Tregzi (also referred to as Orca-T), for use in patients with hematologic malignancies undergoing matched donor hematopoietic stem cell transplant (HSCT) to reduce complications like graft-versus-host disease (GvHD) [18] [19] [20] [30] [43] [46] [49] [51] [53] [59] [62] [74] [76] [87] [237].
**What it means:** This approval provides a new treatment option for a critical patient population facing severe transplant complications, potentially improving outcomes and solidifying the role of advanced cell therapies in oncology [18] [59]. It also positions Orca Bio for potential future growth, with analysts speculating about a potential IPO [59].
**Lenses:** *Scientific & Clinical* - Novel cell therapy for GvHD · *Regulatory* - FDA's continued embrace of advanced therapies · *Commercial* - New market entry for Orca Bio.

### 3. [Medical journal retracts Tavneos pivotal paper amid wider regulatory clampdown](https://pharmaceutical-technology.com/news/medical-journal-retracts-tavneos-pivotal-paper/) {major}
**What happened:** A medical journal has retracted the pivotal paper for Amgen's Tavneos amid regulatory clampdown, with both the FDA and EMA reportedly moving to pull the drug from the market [11] [57] [102] [36].
**What it means:** This represents a major setback for Amgen, raising significant questions about the integrity of clinical data and potentially leading to the withdrawal of a previously approved drug, impacting patients and the company's revenue from the asset [11] [102]. The retraction also highlights heightened scrutiny on clinical trial data and regulatory processes [102].
**Lenses:** *Regulatory & Legal* - Data integrity, potential market withdrawal · *Financial* - Revenue loss for Amgen · *Scientific & Clinical* - Impact on patient access and trust in published data.

### 2. [FDA Approves Two Rare Disease Treatments: Regeneron's Pasatru and Ultragenyx's Genglycos](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/regeneron-ipsen-fda-bone-disease-pasatru-approval/723146/)
**What happened:** The FDA granted approval to Regeneron's Pasatru for fibrodysplasia ossificans progressiva (FOP), an ultra-rare genetic disorder causing bone to form in soft tissues, and gave accelerated approval to Ultragenyx's Genglycos, the first gene therapy for Glycogen Storage Disease Type Ia (GSDIa) [1] [3] [7] [9] [73] [93].
**What it means:** Regeneron's approval sets up a market challenge to Ipsen in a rare indication [1]. For Ultragenyx, Genglycos's approval, with an estimated cost of $2.7 million, is a significant win after prior setbacks, providing momentum and a sellable rare pediatric disease priority review voucher [3] [71]. Both approvals underscore continued regulatory focus on addressing unmet needs in rare diseases.
**Lenses:** *Strategic* - Regeneron enters a new competitive market [1] [62]. *Commercial* - Ultragenyx gains a valuable priority review voucher [3]. *Scientific* - First gene therapy for GSDIa, offering a root-cause treatment [93].

### 3. [Trump Nominates Heidi Overton to Lead FDA Amid Senate Concerns](https://www.npr.org/sections/health-shots/2026/08/20/1271100000/what-to-know-about-dr-heidi-overton-trumps-pick-to-lead-the-fda)

**What it means:** Overton's nomination signals a potential shift in the FDA's regulatory approach, particularly given her background and the political nature of the appointment [4] [11] [57]. The early pushback from the Senate suggests a potentially contentious confirmation process, which could impact the agency's stability and future policy direction [14].
**Lenses:** *Regulatory* - Potential for new leadership to reshape FDA priorities and enforcement [4] [72]. *Leadership* - Political appointment facing immediate congressional scrutiny [14] [27].

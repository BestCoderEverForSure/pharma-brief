### 1. [Bristol Myers Squibb Dips on ‘Confounding’ Lung Drug Results](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bristol-myers-dips-confounding-lung-drug-results)

### 3. [Pharma’s $500B Patent Problem Will Come in Waves — Not as a Cliff](https://www.statnews.com/2026/10/12/pharma-patent-cliff-waves-merck-abbvie-johnson-johnson-sanofi/)

**What it means:** Companies like Merck, AbbVie, Johnson & Johnson, and Sanofi are identified as most vulnerable in the coming years, necessitating strategic pipeline diversification and M&A to offset revenue losses [4].
**Lenses:** *Financial* - Projects significant revenue erosion for major players, impacting future earnings and valuations. *Strategic* - Forces companies to accelerate R&D, pursue acquisitions, and focus on new growth drivers to mitigate the impact. *Regulatory* - Generic competition is a natural outcome of patent expiry, but the scale of this wave is notable.

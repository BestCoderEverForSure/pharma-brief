### 2. AbbVie and Genmab's Epkinly Shows Strong Results in Lymphoma Trial
**What happened:** A dual-acting drug, Epkinly (epcoritamab), developed by AbbVie and Genmab, achieved better-than-expected results in a frontline trial for newly diagnosed diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL) [3].
**What it means:** These positive results could position Epkinly as the "undisputed" new standard of care in frontline DLBCL, according to one analyst. This would intensify competition with existing treatments and potentially reshape treatment paradigms for this aggressive blood cancer, impacting rivals like Johnson & Johnson [3].
**Lenses:** *Scientific & Clinical* - The strong trial data suggests a significant clinical benefit, potentially offering a more effective initial treatment option for DLBCL patients. · *Commercial & Market Access* - If approved, Epkinly could capture substantial market share in a lucrative oncology segment, necessitating strategic pricing and reimbursement efforts [3].

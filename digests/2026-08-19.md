### 2. Amylyx Scores Late-Stage Win with GLP-1 Blocker for Post-Bariatric Hypoglycemia
**What happened:** Amylyx Pharmaceuticals announced positive late-stage trial results for its GLP-1 blocker, avexitide, in patients with post-bariatric surgery hypoglycemia, a rare metabolic condition. The drug effectively regulated blood sugar, and Amylyx plans to file for FDA approval [4] [7] [9] [47] [56] [80] [221].
**What it means:** This success could open a new market for Amylyx in a niche but significant metabolic disorder, leveraging a drug acquired cheaply in a bankruptcy auction. It also highlights the continued expansion of GLP-1 related therapies beyond obesity, into more specific metabolic indications.
**Lenses:** *Scientific & Clinical* - Positive Phase 3 data validates a new mechanism in a specific metabolic condition. *Commercial* - Potential for a new product in a rare disease market, acquired at low cost.

### 3. LEO Pharma and BioMarin Make Strategic Rare Disease Acquisitions
**What happened:** LEO Pharma acquired worldwide rights to Tanabe Pharma's rare skin disease drug dersimelagon for up to $435 million, with the drug already under FDA review for two conditions causing severe reactions to sunlight [3] [13] [179] [190] [194] [206] [207] [210] [213] [232] [239] [246]. Separately, BioMarin acquired an experimental drug for a rare bone disorder in a $275 million deal to bolster its pipeline [1] [67] [120] [127] [142] [172] [208].
**What it means:** These deals underscore a continued strategic focus by established pharma companies on rare disease assets, which often command premium pricing and offer clearer market access pathways. For LEO Pharma, dersimelagon could be a first-in-class oral therapy for erythropoietic protoporphyria (EPP) and X-linked protoporphyria (XLP), challenging existing treatments [210] [232]. BioMarin's acquisition aims to expand its largest addressable patient population in muscle and skeletal diseases [1].
**Lenses:** *Strategic & Competitive* - Companies are actively acquiring late-stage rare disease assets to strengthen pipelines. *Financial & Capital Markets* - Significant deal values reflect the perceived value and market potential of these niche therapies.

**What it means:** This marks a crucial step in expanding access to pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP) in regions heavily impacted by HIV, offering a more affordable and potentially more accessible option than branded versions. ### 3. On-Demand Oral Therapy for Hereditary Angioedema Recommended for NHS Use
**What happened:** The National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE) in the UK has recommended Ekterly, the first on-demand oral pill for hereditary angioedema (HAE) attacks, for use within the NHS [5].
**What it means:** This recommendation provides a significant convenience advantage for HAE patients, allowing for easier self-administration compared to existing injectable treatments. It could lead to improved patient adherence and quality of life, potentially shifting market preference towards oral therapies in this indication [5].
**Lenses:** *Commercial & Market Access* - Improved patient convenience and potential market shift. · *Regulatory & Legal* - NICE recommendation for NHS use. · *Scientific & Clinical* - Oral administration offers a new treatment modality.
